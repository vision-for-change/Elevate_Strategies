FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started with Elevate Strategies.

How long does a website project take?
Most websites are completed within two to four weeks depending on complexity. We provide a detailed timeline at the start of your project so you always know what to expect and when each milestone will be delivered.
Do you work with businesses outside Ottawa?
Absolutely. We work with startups and businesses across Canada and internationally. All collaboration is handled remotely via email, video calls, and project management tools, so location is never a barrier.
What is included in the Website Maintenance plan?
Our maintenance plans cover regular software and plugin updates, security monitoring, speed optimization, minor content edits, and priority email support. Plans start at $15 per month with no lock-in contract required.
Can you help with branding if I already have a logo?
Yes. We can work with your existing logo and build a complete brand system around it, including color palettes, typography guidelines, brand standards documents, and social media templates.
Do you require long-term contracts?
No long-term contracts are required for any of our services. Monthly services like social media management and website maintenance are billed month-to-month and can be paused or cancelled with 30 days notice.
What industries do you specialize in?
We work across a wide range of industries including tech startups, wellness and health, retail and e-commerce, professional services, food and beverage, and local service businesses. If you are a startup or growing business, we are built for you.
